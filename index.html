Mobility

Even though I came from abroad, I see mobility as an essential part of my training. I am eager to undertake an international mobility experience in order to expand my technical skills, immerse myself in a new cultural and academic environment, and build a global professional network.

🇺🇸 United States – Georgia Institute of Technology

Through the partnership that ENSEEIHT maintains with Georgia Tech, I could spend a semester or a year at this world-renowned university, benefiting from a rigorous engineering curriculum and a truly international campus environment. This experience would allow me to deepen my expertise in computer science, improve my English proficiency, and strengthen my ability to thrive in an international engineering context.

🇨🇳 China – Wuhan University

Among ENSEEIHT’s international partners, Wuhan University stands out as a leading institution. Choosing this destination would allow me to engage with a rich and dynamic culture, understand engineering challenges from an Asian perspective, and develop adaptability in a rapidly evolving environment. This opportunity would be particularly valuable given China’s global influence in technological innovation and the potential to collaborate on cross-cultural engineering projects.

Sustainability & Civic Engagement

Through different initiatives, I’ve explored how technology, empathy, and teamwork can contribute to a more responsible and inclusive society. Here are some of the civic and sustainable activities I’ve taken part in:

🤝 Visit to a Retirement Home

With friends, I organized a visit to a local retirement home. We brought clothes and food, spent time listening to residents' stories, played games, and shared moments that highlighted how simple gestures create meaningful connections across generations.

🌍 Fresque du Climat

I participated in the Fresque du Climat workshop, where I learned about the causes and consequences of climate change. It was a collaborative and eye-opening experience that deepened my understanding of environmental challenges and the importance of collective action.

♻️ Atelier “2 Tonnes”

During the 2 Tonnes workshop, I calculated my carbon footprint and worked in a team to create a transition scenario toward a low-carbon future by 2050. This activity helped me better understand both individual and collective levers for environmental transition.

🎭 “Le Procès de King-Kong”

I attended this interactive theater at the Frebentree, ENSEEIHT. The play tackled gender-based and sexual violence through a thought-provoking performance. I participated in the post-play debate, which opened discussions about respect, equality, and the role of art in social awareness.

🎬 Ciné-débat : “Picture a Scientist”

I attended this movie-debate at ENSEEIHT, featuring the documentary “Picture a Scientist.” The screening and discussion highlighted gender inequalities in research and in the same day I attended the Matilda Effect’s presentation — the tendency to overlook women’s scientific contributions. Participating in this event strengthened my awareness of equality issues in STEM fields.

“Through these experiences, I’ve developed empathy, environmental awareness, and a sense of collective responsibility — values I aim to carry forward in my journey as an engineer.”
